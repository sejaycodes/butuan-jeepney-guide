04

De Oro – City Proper

via Davao–Agusan National Highway · JC Aquino Avenue · North Montilla Boulevard

Route 4 (R4) connects De Oro Barangay Hall Off-Street Terminal and the Butuan Integrated Jeepney Terminal, serving commuters traveling between De Oro, Ampayon, and the city proper. The route passes through the Davao–Agusan National Highway, Surigao–Butuan National Highway, and downtown streets, providing access to Ampayon Public Market and major roads within Butuan City. According to the provided ArcGIS route plan, the westbound route measures 15.38 km, while the eastbound route measures 17.32 km.

⚠️ Route Advisory: Terminal Bypass

Route 4 drivers may not enter the Butuan Integrated Jeepney Terminal. Instead, after passing Salvador Calo Street, some drivers proceed directly to Magsaysay Street. Passengers boarding or alighting near the terminal are advised to confirm the actual pickup or drop-off point with the driver before traveling.

⚠️ Route Advisory: Ampayon and De Oro

Although the official Route 4 structure extends to De Oro Barangay Hall Off-Street Terminal via the Davao–Agusan National Highway, many drivers reportedly stop at Ampayon instead of continuing to De Oro. Passengers traveling to De Oro should confirm with the driver whether the trip will continue beyond Ampayon before boarding, as they may need to transfer to another vehicle.

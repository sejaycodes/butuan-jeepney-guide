01

Libertad – City Proper

via J.C. Aquino Avenue

This route serves residents of Libertad and nearby barangays, running end-to-end from the Libertad area to Guingona Park and the downtown commercial district. It's one of the busiest routes in the network, especially during school and market hours.

The Libertad – City Proper route is for those residing in Libertad and select parts of Doongan. The end-to-end of this route runs from the Libertad area until A.D. Curato Street near Gaisano Capital.

⚠️ Eastbound Route Advisory

Some Route 1 eastbound drivers may skip South A.D. Curato Street and instead stop along Ester Luna Street, near Saint Joseph Cathedral, a major passenger pickup point. From there, they proceed to J.C. Aquino Avenue toward Bancasi.

This may reflect the travel pattern used by some drivers from the former Route 1. If you need to pass through South A.D. Curato Street, confirm the route with the driver before boarding.

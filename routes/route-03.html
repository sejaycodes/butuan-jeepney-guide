Ampayon – Libertad

via J.C Aquino Avenue

Route 3 (R3) connects Ampayon and Libertad, passing through the Surigao–Butuan National Highway and J.C. Aquino Avenue. It serves commuters traveling between Ampayon, its public market, and the Libertad area, providing access to key roads and nearby destinations along the corridor. Passengers should confirm the direction and intended drop-off point with the driver before boarding.

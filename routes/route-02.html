02

City Loop via Airport

via J.C Aquino Avenue, Downtown, North Montilla Blvd., and Jose Rosales Avenue

Route 2 (R2) is an intra-city multicab/public utility jeepney route connecting Barangay Dumalagan and Langihan Road / Langihan terminal area. Under the route reorganization implemented in October 2025, R2 took over the number previously assigned to Route 4. Its documented corridor remained essentially unchanged; the route number changed.

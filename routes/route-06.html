06

Maguinda – Provincial Capitol

via Butuan–Las Nieves Road · Villa Kananga · South Montilla Boulevard

Route 6 (R6) connects Maguinda Covered Court Off-Street Terminal and Agusan del Norte Provincial Capitol Off-Street Terminal. The route passes through the Butuan–Las Nieves Road, D Plaza II Avenue, Villa Kananga Road, South Montilla Boulevard, Pizarro Street, T. Guingona Sr. Avenue, and Bonbon–Capitol Drive. It provides a transport connection between Maguinda and the Provincial Capitol area, with a reported ArcGIS route length of 30.59 km.

Route 6 provides a connection between Maguinda and the Agusan del Norte Provincial Capitol area, linking communities along the Butuan–Las Nieves Road with urban roads and the provincial government center. Its alignment through Villa Kananga and the downtown corridor offers commuters access to different parts of Butuan City before reaching the Provincial Capitol terminal. Passengers should confirm the direction of travel and intended terminal with the driver before boarding, particularly when traveling to or from Maguinda.

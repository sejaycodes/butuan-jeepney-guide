05

Santo Niño – City Proper

via Ampayon · J.C. Aquino Avenue · Langihan Road

Route 5 (R5) connects Santo Niño Barangay Hall Off-Street Terminal and the Butuan Integrated Jeepney Terminal, serving commuters traveling between Santo Niño, Ampayon, and the city proper. The route passes through the Surigao–Butuan National Highway, J.C. Aquino Avenue, and several downtown streets, including J. Rosales Avenue, Ochoa Avenue, and Langihan Road. It also provides access to Ampayon Public Market and the Langihan area. Based on the provided ArcGIS route plan, the westbound route measures 20.86 km, while the eastbound route measures 19.97 km.

⚠️ Route Advisory: Eastbound — Santo Niño

Although the official Route 5 eastbound route extends to Santo Niño Barangay Hall Off-Street Terminal, some drivers reportedly stop at Ampayon instead of continuing to Santo Niño. Passengers traveling to Santo Niño are advised to confirm with the driver before boarding, as they may need to transfer to another vehicle to reach their destination.

03

Tungao – Provincial Capitol

via Butuan City–Malaybalay Road · Montilla Boulevard · J. Rosales Avenue

Route 7 (R7) connects Tungao Off-Street Terminal and Agusan del Norte Provincial Capitol Off-Street Terminal. The route follows the Butuan City–Malaybalay Road before passing through Montilla Boulevard, S. Calo Street, and J. Rosales Avenue toward the Provincial Capitol area. It provides a transport connection between Tungao and the city proper, with a reported ArcGIS route length of 32.04 km.

Route 7 serves commuters traveling between Tungao and the Agusan del Norte Provincial Capitol area, connecting communities outside the downtown core with the city's urban road network and provincial government center. Its alignment along the Butuan City–Malaybalay Road and through the downtown corridor provides access to destinations along the way. Passengers are encouraged to confirm the direction of travel and the intended terminal with the driver before boarding, particularly when traveling to or from Tungao.

Base fare

First 4 kilometers. Add ₱1.50 per succeeding kilometer.

Discounts

Students, senior citizens, and PWDs get 20% off the fare, city-wide.

Operating hours

Most routes run 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Availability drops sharply after 8 PM.
